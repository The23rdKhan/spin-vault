# Privacy Policy for Spin Vault

**Last Updated: April 16, 2026**

## Introduction

Spin Vault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**IMPORTANT:** This is a template. Consult with a legal professional before publishing.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you create an account (optional), we may collect:
- Email address (if linking account)
- Display name/username
- Profile picture (if uploaded)
- Google/Apple account ID (for OAuth sign-in)

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use the app, we automatically collect:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Game sessions, spins played, coins earned/spent, achievements unlocked
- **Log Data:** App crashes, errors, performance metrics
- **Analytics:** Time spent in app, features used, screen views

### 3. Transaction Information

For in-app purchases, we collect:
- Purchase history
- Transaction IDs
- Receipt validation data
- Payment status (processed by Apple/Google, not us)

**Note:** We do NOT store credit card numbers or payment information. All payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

1. **Provide the Service**
   - Process spins and award wins
   - Track coin balance and transactions
   - Unlock achievements and level progression
   - Save your game progress

2. **Improve the Service**
   - Analyze gameplay patterns
   - Fix bugs and crashes
   - Optimize performance
   - Develop new features

3. **Communicate with You**
   - Send push notifications (if enabled)
   - Respond to support requests
   - Announce updates and events

4. **Prevent Fraud**
   - Detect unusual betting patterns
   - Validate in-app purchase receipts
   - Prevent account abuse
